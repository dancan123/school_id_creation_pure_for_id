Anti-Counterfeit Protection

Cryptographic QR Verification

Every card carries a unique, tamper-proof QR code. When scanned at access gates or security checkpoints, it verifies the holder in real-time against the institution's official database.

  • Scan Telemetry: Tracks total scan count and last-scanned timestamp for security audit trails.
  • Automated Expiry Checks: Expired or revoked credentials immediately alert security personnel in red.
  • Card Re-issuance Invalidation: Replacing a lost card automatically deactivates the previous QR token.
