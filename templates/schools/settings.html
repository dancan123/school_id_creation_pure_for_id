System Usage Guide
Step-by-step procedures for the ID Generation System
1
Register your institution
Go to Settings and fill in institution name, colors, and upload your logo.
2
Upload stamp and signature
In the Stamp and Signature section, upload a transparent PNG seal. If no stamp is uploaded, the system auto-generates one from your institution name.
3
Add students or staff
Use Students, Add Student or share the Self-Onboarding Link so holders register themselves with a passport photo.
4
Generate ID cards
Use Single ID, select a student, then Generate Card. Or use Bulk Generate for batch operations.
5
Print cards
Download the CR80 PDF (ISO 7810 credit-card size) and print on a CR80 card printer at 300 DPI.

Settings fields explained

FieldPurpose
Institution TypeSwitches field labels. School uses ADM NO, CLASS, STREAM, UPI. Company uses STAFF ID, DEPARTMENT, UNIT, EMP NO.
Authority TitlePrinted under the signature on card back. E.g. Principal, CEO, Bishop, Director.
Card PrefixPrepended to admission number in card field, e.g. STF-001.
Validity YearsCard expiry equals Issue Year plus this number. Triggers renewal workflow when expired.
Guilloche WatermarkAdds micro-wave anti-counterfeiting pattern on both sides. Highly recommended.
Card Title OverrideReplaces the automatic title at the top of the card front.

Add individually via Students, Add Student. Fill in full name, admission number, class, DOB, and upload a passport photo.
2
Bulk import via CSV. Download the sample CSV from Students, Bulk Import, fill in rows, and upload. Photos can be matched by admission number.
3
Approve self-onboarded records from Students, Pending Approvals if auto-approve is off.

Self-onboarding lets students and staff register via a public URL without needing an admin account.

1
Enable self-onboarding and save settings on this page.
2
Copy the shareable link and distribute via WhatsApp, email, or a notice-board QR code.
3
Applicants fill in Full Name, Admission or Staff Number, Class, Date of Birth, and upload a passport photo.
4
If auto-approve is off, review pending submissions under Students, Pending Approvals before generating cards.

Single Card

1
Go to Single ID, search or select a student, choose a template if needed, and click Generate Card.
2
A 300 DPI front and back PNG is rendered and a CR80 print-ready PDF is created. Download from the card record.

Bulk Cards

1
Go to Bulk Generate, filter by class or stream, and click Generate All.
2
A ZIP of individual PDFs is produced, or use Batch Print PDF to get all cards on A4 sheets.

Renewal archives the old card, issues a new QR token and expiry date, while preserving full audit history.

Single Renewal

1
Open the student in Single ID. If an active card exists, click Renew Card, add notes, and confirm.
2
The old card is archived as renewed. The new card shows a RENEWED R1 badge (R2, R3 for subsequent renewals).

Bulk Renewal

1
Go to Bulk Generate and use the Bulk Renew panel. Choose scope: Selected, By Class, By Stream, or All.
2
Add a renewal note, e.g. Annual Renewal 2026, then confirm. All matching cards are renewed in one operation.
1
CR80 Format: Every PDF is sized to ISO 7810 CR80 at 300 DPI, the standard credit-card size used by all ID card printers.
2
Single PDF: Download from the card record and print directly on a CR80 card printer.
3
Batch Print PDF: From Bulk Generate, download to get all cards on A4 sheets with crop marks for sheet-feed printers.
4
Verify with the QR code scanner under Verify to confirm card authenticity before laminating.

The system supports companies, NGOs, churches, hospitals, and any organisation, not just schools.

1
Set Institution Type to Company or Organisation. Field labels auto-switch to corporate terminology.
2
Set the Authority Title such as CEO or HR Director. It appears under the signature on the card back.
3
Use Custom Field Labels to rename any card field, e.g. DEPARTMENT, EMP NO, PAYROLL.
4
Register multiple organisations and switch between them using the school switcher in the top navigation bar. Each has completely independent branding.
